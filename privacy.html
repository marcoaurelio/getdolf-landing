Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Dolf is a disc golf scorekeeping app, currently operated by Marco Aurelio Benites. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we do with it. It is deliberately short, because we collect very little.

What we collect

When you create an account we store your name, email address, and — if you choose to provide them — your phone number, PDGA number, and bag tag number. Your phone number is kept private and is not shown to other players. You can sign in with Apple, Google, or your email address. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address from that provider so we can create your account (see “Google user data” below for exactly what we receive from Google). We never receive your Apple or Google password.

When you play, we store the rounds you take part in: the course, the date, your scores, bag tag results, events and check-ins, and any optional shot detail you choose to record, such as fairways hit, putts, and penalty strokes.

Our servers also receive basic technical information automatically, such as your IP address, device or browser type, and app version, which we use to run and secure the service. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

If you choose to add your date of birth, it is kept private — never shown to other players — and used only for features that depend on age, such as age divisions.

Chat. Messages you send in Dolf chats, and the photos, GIFs and polls you share there, are stored so the people in that chat can see them. Photos are kept in our file storage and can be opened by anyone who has the link to them, so share only what you are comfortable sharing. If you report a message, we store your report; the moderators who review it see the reported message, but not who reported it.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, we store your device's push token so we can send you notifications about your rounds, events and chats. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Google user data

If you choose to sign in with Google, Dolf requests only the basic openid, email, and profile scopes. From Google we receive your name, your email address, your Google account ID, and your profile picture if your Google account has one. We do not request access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar, Drive, or any other Google data.

We use this information only to create your Dolf account and to sign you in. Your name is shown to the other players you play with, as described below. Your profile picture and email address are not shown or shared with other players.

We do not sell Google user data. We do not share it with advertisers or use it for advertising. We do not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. It is shared only with the service providers listed below that we need to run sign-in and store your account, and only for that purpose.

We keep this information for as long as you have a Dolf account. You can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing support@getdolf.com; we will delete your account and the Google user data associated with it. You can also remove Dolf's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Dolf's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Location

Dolf asks for your location only when you use a feature that needs it, and only while you are using the app — never in the background. It is used to sort courses by distance (“Near me”), which happens on your device and is not stored, and, if you choose to pin a course or map its tees and baskets, to record where those features are. We store the coordinates of the course features you record, not a history of where you have been. You can decline, or turn location off at any time in your device settings.

How we use it

Your information is used to run the app: to show live leaderboards to the players on your card, to track bag tag standings and token payouts within your group, to run groups, events and leagues, and to show you your own statistics over time. That is all.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it to build advertising profiles, and Dolf shows no ads.

Who can see your scores

Dolf is a social app. Players on the same card, or in the same group, event, or league, can see your name, scores, standings, and bag tag results for that play — that is the point of a shared scorecard. Your email address and phone number are not shown to other players.

Who we share with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run Dolf, under agreements that limit their use of it: Supabase (database and authentication, including email sign-in), Apple and Google (sign-in, if you use them), Netlify (hosting the web app), Expo / EAS (building and delivering the mobile apps, and delivering push notifications through Apple and Google), and GIPHY (GIF search: when you search for a GIF, the words you type — and, as with any web request, your device's IP address — are sent to GIPHY, whose own privacy policy applies; we do not send your name or account details). We may also disclose information if required by law, or as part of a business transfer, in which case we will notify you.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, on servers that may be located in the United States, and is protected by row-level security rules so accounts can only read the data they are entitled to.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by emailing the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights:

  • California (CCPA/CPRA): the right to know, delete, and correct your information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • Canada (PIPEDA): the right to access and correct the information we hold about you.
  • EU / UK (GDPR): the rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, and port your information, and to withdraw consent. Our legal bases are performing our contract with you, your consent, and our legitimate interest in running and securing the service. You may complain to your local data protection authority.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and its associated data at any time in the app (You → Delete account), or by emailing the address below. We will remove it, though scores already recorded on a shared card may remain visible to that card as an anonymised entry so other players' rounds stay intact.

Children

Dolf is intended for users 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information. If you are under 18, use Dolf only with a parent or guardian's involvement.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, give notice in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or to make a request: support@getdolf.com
