What is Dolf?

Dolf is a disc golf scorekeeping app. Use it to keep score for your rounds, track bag tags, and run clubs, leagues and events — from a casual round with friends to a weekly tag night.

You sign in with Apple, Google or your email address so your scores, tags and stats follow you from round to round. Dolf is free, shows no ads, and runs in your browser at app.getdolf.com.

What it does

Live

One shared card

Add your crew, score hole by hole in honors order, and everyone sees the same leaderboard in real time — even across separate cards in a group.

Tags

Bag tags, handled

Tags redistribute automatically by finish, with the rule built in: they only move if the card finishes with at least two players.

Payouts

Token payouts

Put tokens on the line and Dolf works out the split — a clean ladder, money back for last cash, and ties divided fairly.

Stats

Track your game

Optional shot-by-shot mode logs fairways, penalties and putts, then shows your C1x, C2 and scoring trends round over round.

Works offline, syncs when you're back

Scores queue up when the course has no signal and sync the moment it returns. No lost holes, no arguments on the walk out.
